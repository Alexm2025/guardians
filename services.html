Payroll Outsourcing

An essential business function, handled accurately and on time.

Payroll processing means arriving at the net pay of every employee after the adjustment of necessary taxes and deductions. An efficient payroll process needs care, consistency and up-to-date knowledge of the rules — which is exactly what we provide, end to end.

What’s included

  • Taxes and statutory deductions

03

Statutory Compliance

Statutory compliance in HR is the legal framework an organization must adhere to in dealing with its employees. We keep you legally secure and operationally efficient, with advisory services on the following enactments.

  • The Payment of Wages Act, 1936
  • The Weekly Holidays Act, 1942
  • The Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946
  • The Factories Act, 1948
  • The Employees’ State Insurance Act, 1948
  • The Minimum Wages Act, 1948
  • The Employees’ Provident Funds & Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952
  • The Bombay Labour Welfare Fund Act, 1953
  • The Employment Exchanges (Compulsory Notification of Vacancies) Act, 1959
  • The Maternity Benefit Act, 1961
  • The Apprentices Act (for apprentices appointed for regular nature of work), 1961
  • The Payment of Bonus Act, 1965
  • The Contract Labour (Regulation & Abolition) Act, 1970
  • The Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972
  • The Maharashtra State Tax on Professions, Trades, Callings & Employments Act, 1975
  • The Equal Remuneration Act, 1976
  • The Maharashtra Private Security Guards (Regulation of Employment and Welfare) Act, 1981
  • The Maharashtra Workmen’s Minimum House-Rent Allowance Act, 1983
  • The Building & Other Construction Workers (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act, 1996
  • The Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Act, 2005
  • The Employee’s Compensation Act, 2010
  • The Maharashtra Shops & Establishments (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act, 2017
