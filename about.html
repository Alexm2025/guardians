About us

People who understand both sides of hiring.

Guardians has been offering HR and recruitment solutions in India and abroad for more than a decade.

Who we are

An end-to-end HR solutions firm, headquartered in Thane.

Guardians has successfully placed over 1,000 candidates across more than 10 states, and provides HR solutions to clients in sectors such as travel and tourism, construction, pharma, hospitals, retail, media, marketing, e-commerce, hospitality, logistics, consulting, engineering and manufacturing.

We work with both employers and candidates, and that is why we understand both sides of the recruitment process. Our dedicated team of recruiters, headhunters and consultants pays attention to even the smallest details to create efficient employer–candidate connections.

We take the intricate and diversified HR needs of our clients and turn them into strategic, reliable HR solutions. By aligning your business strategy with our talent strategy, we provide workforce solutions capable of taking your business forward.

By partnering with our clients, we expand their business potential through executive search, middle-management hiring and recruitment process outsourcing.

Why partner with Guardians

Recruitment is one of our driving forces, but not the only one. We go beyond it with orientation, training and complete HRMS solutions that make each new hire’s transition into your organization as smooth as possible.

  • Highly skilled recruiters and consultants with strong domain experience

What drives us

Mission & vision

Our mission

To be the most preferred and reliable end-to-end HR solutions company — handling recruitment, manpower requirements, HR consulting, training and development, and much more. To achieve it, we focus on exceptional service, quality staffing and prompt customer support, working closely with clients to make sure their requirements are met and looking after the job seekers who partner with us.

Our vision

To deliver a multi-dimensional, tailored approach to HR solutions that is unlike anything else in India. Our team of HR experts brings years of industry experience to give clients excellence in service — whether in recruitment, executive search, talent acquisition, or learning and development.

Our commitment

We are a team of professionals committed to quality recruitment and HR consulting services that add real value.

  • Long-term partnerships built on understanding your unique business needs

  • Payroll and statutory compliance that keep you legally secure and operationally efficient
